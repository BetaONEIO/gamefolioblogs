At Gamefolio, we're building a platform where gamers, streamers and creators can showcase their content, build their gaming identity, connect with communities and discover new opportunities.

Competitive gaming is a huge part of that story.

Every clutch round, every last-second save and every tournament run is a moment worth showing off, and Godalions creates plenty of them.

## Welcome to Godalions

Godalions is an esports organisation built around competing, growing its players and bringing its community along for the ride.

From Rocket League to VALORANT, the Godalions crest has shown up across multiple competitive scenes, with rosters taking on tournaments and circuits against teams at every level.

Behind the rosters is a community that tunes in to watch, cheer on and celebrate every win, and that's exactly the kind of energy we want around Gamefolio.
