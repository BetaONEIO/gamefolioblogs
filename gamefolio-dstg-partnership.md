## What is Dark Solar Tactical Gaming?

Dark Solar Tactical Gaming is an inclusive gaming community built around solidarity, mental health awareness, and shared growth. It was founded to counter something a lot of gamers know all too well — the isolation and toxicity that can creep into online spaces — by creating a safe haven where mental health comes first and creators actively lift one another up.

Rather than being built around a single title, DSTG welcomes players across every genre and skill level, from high-stakes shooters and deep RPGs to casual indies, alongside the streamers and content creators who bring those communities to life. The organisation is built on three core pillars:

* **Mental health and well-being** — with wellness check-ins built directly into their Discord community
* **Creator collaboration** — supporting streamers through cross-platform collaboration, multi-stream events, and resource sharing
* **Inclusive squad dynamics** — a zero-tolerance approach to toxicity, so every member feels welcome regardless of platform, genre, or skill level

Members can also build out site profiles, earn XP, and unlock achievements, with live streaming integration through VPZONE.TV and a growing lineup of branded merchandise and affiliate partnerships. It's a community built for the long haul, not just the highlight reel.
