## What Does VPZONE Offer?

### Low-Latency Streaming

VPZONE runs on low-latency LL-HLS delivery with sub-two-second latency, using multi-region origins across North America and Europe.

Creators can broadcast over RTMP or SRT, so existing OBS setups connect without a rebuild.

For anyone doing interactive content, reacting to chat in near real time changes how a stream feels for the people watching it.

### Real-Time Chat and Community Tools

Chat comes with emotes, presence indicators and moderation tools, alongside followers, subscriptions and per-channel controls.

Creators keep ownership of their community rather than renting an audience.

That's the same principle behind Gamefolio: your content, your identity, your community.

### Monetisation From Day One

This is where VPZONE stands out.

The VPZONE Creator Program scales revenue splits as creators grow, starting at 75% for Affiliates, rising to 80% for Verified creators and reaching up to 90% for top creators.

Payouts run daily through Stripe Connect across 26 countries, following a standard chargeback hold period.

Smaller creators typically have to grind for a long time before monetisation is even an option elsewhere. VPZONE opens that door much earlier.

### Clips and Discovery

VPZONE has a dedicated clips section, along with trending categories, creator spotlights and following lists to help viewers find new channels.

Clips are one of the most effective ways for a stream to reach people who weren't watching live, and they're also exactly the kind of content Gamefolio is built to showcase.

### Mobile and Developer Access

The VPZONE mobile app lets viewers discover channels, watch broadcasts and clips and join chat, while eligible creators can broadcast directly from a Mobile Broadcast Studio.

For developers and tool builders, VPZONE offers a public REST API, embeds and OAuth 2.1, which is why the platform already appears in third-party creator tools such as HELIX Cross Talk Ultra.
