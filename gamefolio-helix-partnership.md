## What Does HELIX Offer?

### HELIX Hub

HELIX Hub acts as an all-in-one streaming dashboard, bringing a range of creator tools into one place.

Streamers can access browser-based stream alerts, interactive games, viewer engagement tools, Discord integration, streaming assets and other tools designed to help improve their broadcasts and communities.

For creators who are starting to take streaming more seriously, having these tools available from one dashboard can make managing a channel considerably easier.

### HELIX Music

Music can be one of the biggest headaches when creating live and recorded content.

HELIX Music provides stream-safe, royalty-free music designed for content creators, helping streamers avoid the copyright issues that can lead to muted VODs, claims or takedowns.

It also includes curated playlists and viewer song requests, giving creators another way to make their streams more interactive.

For Gamefolio creators regularly turning streams into clips, highlights and other content, having VOD-safe music can be particularly valuable.

### HELIX Cross Talk Ultra

If you stream across multiple platforms, keeping track of everything happening can quickly become complicated.

HELIX Cross Talk Ultra brings chats and events from platforms including Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Rumble, Blaze and VPZone into a unified OBS overlay.

That means creators can keep an eye on conversations, follows, subscriptions, raids, donations and other activity without constantly jumping between different platforms.

### HELIX Sync

Creating content is one thing. Uploading and distributing it everywhere is another.

HELIX Sync helps automate content distribution between YouTube and Rumble, including videos, Shorts and recorded livestreams.

It's designed to reduce the amount of repetitive work creators have to do when maintaining a presence across multiple platforms.

### HELIX Connect

This is an area that particularly aligns with what we're building at Gamefolio.

HELIX Connect is a creator marketplace connecting streamers with brands and game developers looking for creators to work with.

Creators can discover partnership and sponsorship opportunities, showcase their channel metrics and connect with companies interested in reaching their audiences.

Gamefolio is also developing new ways for creators to connect with indie developers and gaming opportunities through features such as our Bounty Hub, so there's a clear shared vision between our two platforms.
