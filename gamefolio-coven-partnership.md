We're excited to announce our partnership with The Coven, the stream team founded by [GothicSkullMum](https://www.gothicskullmum.com) and a community built for the misfits, outcasts, weirdos and night owls of gaming.

At Gamefolio, we're building a platform where gamers, streamers and creators can showcase their content, build their gaming identity, connect with communities and discover new opportunities.

But platforms only matter because of the people on them.

And some of the best people in gaming are the ones who found each other late at night, in a chat that felt like home.

That's what [The Coven](https://www.gothicskullmum.com/the-coven/) is.

## Welcome to The Coven

In their own words, The Coven is "a gathering of gamers, streamers, creators, and wandering souls who found a place to belong together."

It was built as a home for anyone who has ever felt like they didn't quite fit in elsewhere, and it runs on kindness, friendship, humour and genuine connection.

Most importantly, it's a place where established streamers and people just starting out stand side by side.

"Here, you are welcomed exactly as you are."

That's a line we'd be proud to put on any community, and it's exactly the kind of energy we want around Gamefolio.

## Why Gamefolio Has Partnered With The Coven

Gamefolio has always been about more than uploading gaming clips.

We want to give creators a permanent home for their clips, streams, achievements and gaming identity, and help them get discovered along the way.

The Coven already lives that idea. Members link their Gamefolio profiles right alongside their streaming channels on The Coven's team page, putting their highlights in one place for their community to find.

It creates a natural connection:

**Stream with The Coven. Showcase on Gamefolio. Grow across both.**

## A Partnership Built on Community

As Gamefolio continues to grow, we want to stand alongside communities that genuinely look after their people.

The Coven fits that perfectly.

Both of us exist because gaming is better when you're not doing it alone, and because smaller creators deserve a space where they're supported rather than overlooked.

If you're looking for somewhere to hang out after dark, catch a horror run, unwind with something cozy or just find people who get it, The Coven is well worth a visit.

And this is only the beginning of what we hope to do together.

We're excited to welcome The Coven as an official Gamefolio partner and look forward to finding new ways for our communities to connect, create and grow.
